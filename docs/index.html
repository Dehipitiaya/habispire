Common questions

How do I restore Premium?

Sign in with the same Google account and choose Restore purchases in the app.

How do I cancel a subscription?

Open Google Play and go to Payments & subscriptions › Subscriptions. Select Habispire to manage or cancel your plan.

How do I turn off usage reports?

Open Settings › Privacy & data in Habispire and switch off Share anonymous usage data.
