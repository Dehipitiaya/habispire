Habispire Privacy Policy

First effective: 06 Nov 2026 · Last updated: 28 Nov 2026

This policy explains what information the Habispire app for Android (the "App") handles, why, and the choices you have. The App is provided by Anuththara Dehipitiya ("we", "us", "our"), based in Sri Lanka. You can reach us at habispire.support@gmail.com.

In short: your habits stay on your phone. We do not run accounts, show ads or sell data. Google sign-in lets you use Premium and back up habits to your own Google Drive. After setup, the App sends a daily usage report identified by a random installation ID while sharing is enabled. You can turn this off in Settings > Privacy & data. We also receive purchase information from Google Play when the App verifies a Premium purchase.

1. Information that stays on your phone

Everything you enter is stored on your device: habit names, notes, categories, icons, schedules, reminder times, daily progress and completion history, timer sessions, and your settings (theme, colour, font, sounds and so on). Your individual habit records stay on your phone and in the backups described below. Section 4 explains which settings and habit totals the usage report includes.

Android may include the App's data in your phone's normal Google backup (Android Auto Backup) if you have that feature turned on in your phone's settings. That backup is handled by Google under your Google account and Google's terms. It is separate from the optional Drive backup in section 2.

2. Optional Google sign-in and Drive backup

Google sign-in is optional for basic habit tracking. The App requires it for Premium access and for Google Drive backup. If you sign in, the App receives your Google account’s name, email address, profile picture and account identifier. The App uses this information to show your profile, enable Premium access while you’re signed in, and run the backup you choose to enable. They are stored on your phone and are not sent to us.

If you turn on Sync, the App saves a copy of your habits and history to a hidden folder in your own Google Drive (the "app data folder", using the drive.appdata permission, which lets the App see only its own hidden folder and none of your other files). You can also turn on Back up app settings to include supported preferences such as theme, accent colour, font and chart options. The copy is encrypted on your phone before it is uploaded, so the file is not readable as plain text. The encryption key is derived from your Google account identifier so that a new phone signed in to the same account can restore it. This protects the file against casual exposure; it does not hide it from Google or from anyone who controls your Google account. We do not have access to your Drive and never see the backup.

You can turn Sync off or sign out in Settings. The existing backup stays in your Drive until you delete it: open Google Drive on the web, choose Settings > Manage apps, find Habispire and choose Delete hidden app data.

3. Notifications, alarms and device features

The App uses the Android permissions and system features listed below.

  • Notifications: to show habit reminders and timer alerts.
  • Reminder scheduling: the App uses Android’s inexact alarms. Android may delay reminders and timer alerts, especially when your phone is idle.
  • Run at startup: to re-arm your reminders after the phone restarts.
  • Vibration: for timer alerts.
  • Internet: for Google sign-in, Drive backup, purchases and the usage report.

Home-screen widgets show information from your habits on your phone only. No location, contacts, photos, microphone or camera access is requested.

4. Usage analytics (on by default)

New installs have sharing selected by default. You can review the choice during setup. The App sends a daily usage report after setup while Share anonymous usage data is on. You can turn it off in Settings > Privacy & data. Turning it off stops future reports and clears the installation ID, usage counters and stored acquisition category from your phone. Reports already sent follow the retention policy below. Google Play purchase verification works separately.

The report contains:

  • A random identifier created on your phone. It is not linked to your name, email, Google account, phone number or any hardware identifier, and a new one is created if you turn sharing off and on again.
  • Your choices in the App: theme, accent colour, font, glass or plain look, sort order, chart type, timer and sound settings, whether you are signed in, and whether Drive sync is on.
  • Totals about your habits, never their content: how many habits you have, how many of each kind (yes/no, count, time, quit) and schedule (daily, weekly, monthly), how many have reminders, notes or categories, how many different categories, which icon pack, and counts of recent check-ins, average completion rate and streak lengths.
  • The number of widgets placed by type and the number using each accent colour, including default colours and automatic palettes.
  • Feature counters, including app opens, screens visited, habits created, check-ins, countdown and interval timer starts, reminder actions, widget opens, Premium screen views and checkout starts by plan.
  • Whether notification and exact-alarm permissions are allowed, and whether Premium is active.
  • Basic device and app information, including app version, install date and installer, Android version, device manufacturer, rounded screen size, screen density, tablet and low-memory indicators, language, locale region and time zone.
  • For installs from Google Play, a broad acquisition channel and source when available, such as organic Google Play, paid social, email or a recognised referral source. The App reads the Google Play Install Referrer on the phone and sends only the resulting category labels.

The report never contains habit names, notes, category names, habit or widget instance IDs, your Google account or Drive contents, your location, contacts or photos. Raw referral links, advertising click identifiers and referral timestamps stay off our server.

We use reports to understand feature use and improve the App. We also compare acquisition channels in aggregate to measure how people find Habispire, including how paid promotions perform. Vercel Inc. and Neon, Inc. process the reports for us over encrypted connections (HTTPS). We do not sell reports or share them with advertising partners. We keep reports for up to 24 months, then delete them or reduce them to anonymous totals.

Where applicable, we rely on our legitimate interests in understanding and improving the App for product analytics. You can object to usage analytics or stop sharing in Settings.

5. Purchases (Premium)

Some features require Habispire Premium, bought as a subscription or a one-time purchase through Google Play. Google Play processes payments and handles your card or bank details. The App asks Google Play whether your account has an active purchase so it can unlock Premium, and stores that status on your phone. Google's own privacy policy applies to purchases.

The App sends the purchase token to our server so we can confirm your purchase with Google Play, prevent fraud and help with payment problems. We store a hashed copy of the token, the product, the purchase status and Google Play’s order number. When Google Play makes them available, we also store the billing country, subscription plan and offer, and order dates, order status (including refunds), the original amount charged, currency and the revenue Google Play reports for the order. We use these fields to report on sales and plan popularity and understand demand by country.

The order number lets us find a purchase in Google Play Console, where Google may make additional order or buyer information available to developers. Our analytics database stores the purchase fields described above. If there is a payment problem, we may attach a short message to the purchase for the App to display. We keep purchase records while the purchase is active and for up to 24 months afterwards.

6. Advertising and data sharing

  • The App has no ads. We measure how people find Habispire as described in section 4.
  • We operate our own analytics and use Google Play Install Referrer to get acquisition information on the phone.
  • No sale or "sharing" of personal information for advertising.
  • No collection of precise location, contacts, photos, files, messages or microphone/camera data.

8. Your choices and rights

  • Usage data: switch it off in Settings > Privacy & data.
  • Your habits: edit or delete them in the App; deleting the App removes the data on your phone.
  • Google account data: sign out in Settings, and delete the hidden Drive data as described in section 2.
  • Permissions: change or withdraw them in your phone's system settings.

Depending on where you live (for example the EEA, UK, California, Sri Lanka and other places with data protection laws), you may have the right to access, correct, delete or export personal information we hold about you, to object to or restrict its use, and to complain to your data protection authority. Usage reports are filed under a random installation ID, so we may be unable to find them by name or email. If your request concerns a Premium purchase, a Google Play order number can help us find the record. You can contact us using the address below. We will not treat you differently for using these rights.

9. Children

The App is not directed to children under 13 (or under 16 where that is the local age of digital consent), and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has given us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

10. Security

Data on your phone is protected by Android's app sandbox and your device lock. Data sent to Google or to us travels over encrypted connections. No system is perfectly secure, so please keep your phone and Google account protected.

11. Changes to this policy

We publish policy updates here with a new last-updated date. For significant changes, we will explain in the App what is changing, when it takes effect and what choices you have. If a new use of your data requires consent, we will ask for it separately before that use begins.
