Habispire Privacy Policy

Effective date: 06 Nov 2026 · Last updated: 06 Nov 2026

This policy explains what information the Habispire app for Android (the "App") handles, why, and the choices you have. The App is provided by Anuththara Dehipitiya ("we", "us", "our"), based in Sri Lanka. You can reach us at habispire.support@gmail.com.

In short: your habits stay on your phone. We do not run accounts, show ads or sell data. If you choose, you can sign in with Google to back up your habits to your own Google Drive. By default the App also sends us a small anonymous usage report, which you can switch off at any time in Settings > Privacy & data.

1. Information that stays on your phone

Everything you enter is stored on your device: habit names, notes, categories, icons, schedules, reminder times, daily progress and completion history, timer sessions, and your settings (theme, colour, font, sounds and so on). We do not receive this information, and it is not part of the anonymous report in section 4.

Android may include the App's data in your phone's normal Google backup (Android Auto Backup) if you have that feature turned on in your phone's settings. That backup is handled by Google under your Google account and Google's terms. It is separate from the optional Drive backup in section 2.

2. Optional Google sign-in and Drive backup

Signing in is optional. If you sign in with Google, the App receives your Google account's name, email address, profile picture and a permanent account identifier. These are used only to show your profile in Settings and to run the backup. They are stored on your phone and are not sent to us.

If you turn on Sync, the App saves a copy of your habits and history to a hidden folder in your own Google Drive (the "app data folder", using the drive.appdata permission, which lets the App see only its own hidden folder and none of your other files). The copy is encrypted on your phone before it is uploaded, so the file is not readable as plain text. The encryption key is derived from your Google account identifier so that a new phone signed in to the same account can restore it. This protects the file against casual exposure; it does not hide it from Google or from anyone who controls your Google account. We do not have access to your Drive and never see the backup.

You can turn Sync off or sign out in Settings. The existing backup stays in your Drive until you delete it: open Google Drive on the web, choose Settings > Manage apps, find Habispire and choose Delete hidden app data.

3. Notifications, alarms and device features

The App asks for these permissions, only for the features described:

  • Notifications: to show habit reminders and timer alerts.
  • Alarms (exact alarms): to deliver reminders at the time you choose.
  • Run at startup: to re-arm your reminders after the phone restarts.
  • Vibration: for timer alerts.
  • Internet: for Google sign-in, Drive backup, purchases and the anonymous report.

Home-screen widgets show information from your habits on your phone only. No location, contacts, photos, microphone or camera access is requested.

4. Anonymous usage data (on by default)

To understand how the App is used and to improve it, the App sends us one small anonymous report per day while Share anonymous usage data is on. You can turn it off at any time in Settings > Privacy & data. Turning it off stops the reports and deletes the random identifier and counters stored on your phone.

The report contains:

  • A random identifier created on your phone. It is not linked to your name, email, Google account, phone number or any hardware identifier, and a new one is created if you turn sharing off and on again.
  • Your choices in the App: theme, accent colour, font, glass or plain look, sort order, chart type, timer and sound settings, whether you are signed in, and whether Drive sync is on.
  • Totals about your habits, never their content: how many habits you have, how many of each kind (yes/no, count, time, quit) and schedule (daily, weekly, monthly), how many have reminders, notes or categories, how many different categories, which icon pack, and counts of recent check-ins, average completion rate and streak lengths.
  • How many widgets of each type you have placed.
  • How often features were used (for example app opens, screens visited, habits created, check-ins, timer starts, reminder actions), as counters.
  • Whether notification and exact-alarm permissions are allowed, and whether Premium is active.
  • Basic device and app information: app version and install date, how the App was installed (for example Google Play), Android version, device manufacturer (not the model), screen size (rounded) and density, language, region and time zone.

The report never contains habit names, notes, category names, your Google account or Drive contents, your location, contacts or photos.

The report is sent over an encrypted connection (HTTPS) to a server we operate on cloud hosting and database services provided by Vercel Inc. and Neon, Inc., which process it for us. We do not use it for advertising, we do not sell it and we do not share it with anyone else. Because it is not linked to you, we cannot tell which person a report belongs to. We keep reports for up to 24 months and then delete them or reduce them to anonymous totals.

Why we do this (for people in the EEA, UK and similar regions): our legitimate interest in understanding and improving the App, with minimal data and an easy way to opt out. You can object at any time by switching sharing off.

5. Purchases (Premium)

Some features require Habispire Premium, bought as a subscription or a one-time purchase through Google Play. Payment is handled entirely by Google Play; we never see or store your card or payment details. The App asks Google Play whether your account has an active purchase so it can unlock Premium, and stores that status on your phone. Google's own privacy policy applies to purchases.

6. What we do not do

  • No advertising and no advertising identifiers.
  • No third-party analytics or tracking software inside the App.
  • No sale or "sharing" of personal information for advertising.
  • No collection of precise location, contacts, photos, files, messages or microphone/camera data.

8. Your choices and rights

  • Usage data: switch it off in Settings > Privacy & data.
  • Your habits: edit or delete them in the App; deleting the App removes the data on your phone.
  • Google account data: sign out in Settings, and delete the hidden Drive data as described in section 2.
  • Permissions: change or withdraw them in your phone's system settings.

Depending on where you live (for example the EEA, UK, California, Sri Lanka and other places with data protection laws), you may have the right to access, correct, delete or export personal information we hold about you, to object to or restrict its use, and to complain to your data protection authority. Because we hold no information that identifies you, we usually cannot look up a person's data; if you think we hold something about you, write to us and we will help as far as we can. We will not treat you differently for using these rights.

9. Children

The App is not directed to children under 13 (or under 16 where that is the local age of digital consent), and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has given us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

10. Security

Data on your phone is protected by Android's app sandbox and your device lock. Data sent to Google or to us travels over encrypted connections. No system is perfectly secure, so please keep your phone and Google account protected.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the App changes. We will post the new version at this address with a new "last updated" date and, for significant changes, tell you in the App. Using the App after a change means you accept the updated policy.
